Welcome to DYNAMIC lab @ DGIST EECS

Dynamic Machine Intelligence Lab

Building intelligent systems that perceive, reason, and act
in dynamic environments.

The DYNAMIC Lab is led by Prof. Jungin Park in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at DGIST. Our research explores spatiotemporal dynamics for computer vision and video understanding, modality dynamics for multimodal learning, and viewpoint and movement dynamics for embodied AI for machines that can understand and interact with the world.

We value curiosity, rigorous thinking, and open collaboration. The lab is preparing to grow, and student recruiting will open soon.
