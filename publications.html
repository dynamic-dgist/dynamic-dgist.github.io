Publications

In Computer Vision and Machine Learning fields, as opposed to many other disciplines, papers published in top conferences (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICLR, and ICML) are regarded as more important and influential than most SCI journals in general. For example, CVPR is the 2nd rank among all academic fields according to google scholar metrics. Oral or spotlight presentations have highly competitive acceptance rates, about < 2%.
